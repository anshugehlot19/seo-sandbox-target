# Integrating FlowMetrics with Your Existing Stack

Adding FlowMetrics to your B2B tech stack is seamless and non-disruptive. Follow this guide to get up and running in minutes.

## Step 1: Authentication

Connect your identity provider using OAuth 2.0 or SAML for enterprise SSO.

## Step 2: API Integration

Use our RESTful API or Webhook endpoints to sync data from your existing tools.

## Step 3: Dashboard Configuration

Customize your dashboards to show the metrics that matter most to your team.

## Step 4: Team Onboarding

Invite team members and set permission levels based on roles and responsibilities.

## Common Integrations

- **CRM**: Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive
- **Analytics**: Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude
- **Customer Success**: Intercom, Zendesk, Freshdesk
- **Payment**: Stripe, Braintree, PayPal

## Best Practices

- Start with core metrics before onboarding additional data sources
- Use webhooks for real-time updates on critical events
- Set up automated reports to keep stakeholders informed
- Regularly audit your data connections for accuracy

## Conclusion

FlowMetrics integrates with over 50 popular B2B tools, making it easy to consolidate your data landscape and gain a unified view of your business health.
