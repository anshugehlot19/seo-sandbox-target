# 5 KPIs Every B2B SaaS Company Should Track

Building a successful B2B SaaS company requires tracking the right metrics. Here are the five most critical KPIs that every founder and executive should monitor.

## 1. Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)

MRR is the lifeblood of any subscription business. It provides a predictable view of revenue streams and helps forecast future growth.

## 2. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

Understanding how much it costs to acquire a new customer is essential for optimizing marketing spend and ensuring sustainable growth.

## 3. Lifetime Value (LTV)

LTV tells you how much revenue a customer will generate over their entire relationship with your company. The LTV/CAC ratio is a key indicator of business health.

## 4. Churn Rate

Churn measures the percentage of customers who cancel their subscription over a given period. High churn can signal product-market fit issues.

## 5. Activation Rate

The percentage of new users who experience the core value of your product within their first few days. Improving activation rate often has an immediate impact on retention and expansion.

## Conclusion

Focus on these five KPIs to build a data-driven culture and make informed decisions that drive sustainable B2B SaaS growth.
